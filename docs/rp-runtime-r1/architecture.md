## Narration boundary

`RPNarrativeInput` contains committed/public facts and separate sourced public-presentation cues only. Accepted NPC speech requires a frozen `speaker_said` observation for the controlled player, both in the current turn and in the cross-turn window; sharing a place or session alone is insufficient. An accepted NPC visual expression enters by Event ID and requires a witnessed observation. For activity and movement, the narrator reconstructs the player's visual access at the source event sequence from committed movement, zone-choice and perception-link events. This history view does not use today's position to reinterpret an older turn and does not add an RP world state. Fact IDs and authored style provenance remain separate.

Fresh model output is now a closed composition plan. Each local `fact_ref` must appear exactly once in committed order; atoms choose only the supplied `plain`, `compact`, `contextual` or speech-only `dialogue` templates, and groups choose `inline` or `lines`. The server renders actor, target, speech and action together. Refusal keeps an explicit server-owned refusal marker even if its words alone are ambiguous. Exact utterances stay data, including punctuation and token-looking text. Unknown/extra/duplicate/null keys, invalid templates and missing/duplicate/reordered facts are rejected before any emission. Existing deterministic fallback, privacy and source authority remain in force.

Supported expression choices are currently limited to grouping and sourced deterministic POV, tense, place/time and density framing. `plain` retains supported requested style; `compact` selects concise framing and `contextual` selects standard/long framing. Public character style and custom prose instructions may guide these choices only. Free sentence rewriting, character diction and model-created connective prose are not implemented by this version, despite the compatibility provider kind `full_prose`. A fixed capability warning and explicit composition version distinguish fresh limited output from saved legacy prose. Concise/standard/long tests prove supported framing differences on fact-rich input, not literary quality.

Earlier Step output exposed unsupported invented player gaze/intent in sparse speech-plus-silence scenes. Those negative samples and legacy fact guards remain diagnostic regression evidence. They do not authorize current free prose. The closed composition cannot supply new gaze, intent, dialogue or scene objects at all; it renders only public facts, with no private decision input.

Legacy draft diagnostics retain the delimiter-stack parser and one-pass `quote_token` expansion, including nested quotation and literal reference cases. Their regex-based actor checks did not prove attribution: a speaker swap and collapse of distinct events with equal utterances were reproduced against the old validator. Fresh raw/tokenized prose is therefore rejected even if those legacy heuristics pass. Already persisted legacy prose remains readable unchanged and unversioned; it is not silently reclassified or regenerated.

Composition receipts are presentation metadata, not world Events. Migration 077 stores independent ordered fact IDs and paragraph groups on canonical and variant rows. Save checks the authoritative input; restart/history/selection/replay check ordered unique coverage against the independent stored sequence, and saved canonical reads also compare rebuilt authorized facts. A changed group cannot validate itself. Legacy defaults stay empty. Each streamed paragraph cites its actual group; completion carries version and groups. No model call is needed to replay a saved artifact. Optional `CORERP_NARRATIVE_DEBUG` logs only a fixed validation category. NPC semantic quality and literary quality remain unproven experience requirements.
