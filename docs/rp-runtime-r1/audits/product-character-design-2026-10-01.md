## Current flow and why legality is insufficient

The builder binds actor/current speaker to committed personal hearing (`storage/rp_decision.go:58–79`), loads authored persona and directed relationship/readiness (lines 100–135,416–449), global recent dialogue (lines 172–203), relevant exchange/private candidates (204–210), and peer-specific heard-player excerpts (216–228). Those excerpts preserve the player's words but not necessarily the NPC's answer or qualification. Persona missing is MISSING; undeclared relation is UNKNOWN, not automatic stranger hostility. `core/rp_decision.go:38–39` blocks missing persona/address, while intentionally unknown strangers may interact.

Selection and provider identity masking retain authorization and byte limits. Restored recent groups are atomic at priority 0, ordinary relevant groups at 3 and private sketches at 4 (`core/rp_context_selection.go:63–98`). A historical private thought has an applied Event/time/peer and checked hash (`storage/rp_private_decision.go:23–49`); that does not mean it is a currently active intention. Legality and reference membership do not enforce semantic relevance, fresh contribution, personality or factual entailment. The instruction describes those expectations (`decision/chat.go:174–182`), but cannot turn bounded absent history into memory.

Initiative uses the existing wait trigger and controlled player association, actual visibility and sourced opportunities (`storage/rp_initiative.go:44–67,81–95`). Baseline initiative follows sourced needs, work/visit/weather and observed social state; it may legitimately choose silence (`core/rp_initiative.go:30–79`). No sampled conversational reply proves self-initiation. Repeated generic reassurance during player turns is a voice/contribution problem; it is not proof that the wait scheduler failed.

## Smallest structural change

### 1. Reserve evidence for the actual directed peer

Extend the **same** authorized context read with a selection reason, not a new memory narrative. Populate existing `RelevantDialogue` with up to two latest complete authorized exchanges tied to the current actor/interlocutor, even when no query terms match. Preserve exact dialogue, Event IDs, actor, source time and ordering. The ordinary global recent/topic paths remain supplemental. For elapsed-time triggers, select this peer recall from actual prior conversation without fabricating player speech.

An eligible exchange contains a personally heard utterance from the peer and the actor's own causally grouped reply, if any. A peer question without an actor reply may be selected as an unanswered **utterance**, not labeled a complete question/answer pair. Witnessed group siblings keep their individual hearing eligibility; an overheard C utterance must not become a private conversation between A and the NPC merely because they were in one session. Preserve all authorized group members needed for interpretation, with explicit selection subject/peer; do not reconstruct unheard siblings. Use parent-turn/session grouping for words, but actual speaker plus actor-owned response lineage for peer association. Current committed player speech already in the trigger must not be reclassified as historical exchange.

Apply the peer predicate before the candidate limit. A reasonable initial bounded policy reserves 128 of the existing 512 candidate slots for peer-linked history and the remaining 384 for global/topic history, deduplicating Event IDs before grouping. Output still uses four groups/6000 runes: repaired recent groups first, then two reserved peer groups newest-first, then lexical history with remaining capacity. Group completeness is checked against authorized source membership at head, not inferred from a truncated candidate window. If this ratio prevents a complete peer group from fitting, report omission; do not assert comprehensive memory. The 128/384 split is a proposed engineering default, not measured tuning.

Select the latest **one** actor-owned applied private sketch associated with that peer before the global-three limit, using the same committed command/attempt, approved hash and complete-batch/head checks. Union it with the existing latest-three chronology by decision ID, giving at most four historical sketches rather than raising global memory without purpose. Use existing peer/time attribution to identify its role in the packet; if explicit role metadata is needed, add a selection reason, not `current_intent=true`. Non-peer global sketches may inform actor-wide recent mood, but their relationship stance cannot be transplanted to A. A new explicit A sketch supersedes A's historical recency for selection; it does not erase canonical role or create intent lifecycle.

### 2. Make boundedness visible and keep authority separate

Use atomic selected exchange units through builder, actual provider projection and final budget selection. Count upstream candidate/rune exclusions separately from final byte omissions; zero returned rows with a bound hit means incomplete recall, not proof that the NPC never said something. Add counts/status to existing private `ContextSelection`, including peer sketch omission and peer dialogue omission. No public private-memory diagnostic or private rationale should enter Play/narration. If required canon fits but peer recall cannot, keep the legal decision path with explicit context omissions and cautious uncertainty; do not falsify certainty or invent missing words.

Use the existing ledger on every read at a fixed head; no generated synopsis, mutable character profile, embedding provider or secondary world state. At restart, derive the same candidate selection and input hash from the same immutable rows. A disposable index may help measured query cost later, but is not part of this initial design. Version input/selection semantics so staged/replayed old decisions keep their recorded hash contract. Do not rewrite old private proposal JSON or legacy public records.

### 3. Present a character's next contribution using received evidence

Keep the existing v3 private/observable response wire. Give the provider a short role/task order: current speech/trigger → current scene/typed outcomes → authored voice/role/address → selected peer exchange and historical private stance → other permitted evidence. Earlier reassurance is something already said, not an instruction to repeat it. A reply should address the new meaning and may ask one grounded question, offer a sourced topic, accept/refuse or remain silent. Authored temperament determines manner; no canned “progress” line, mandatory new goal, forced movement or forced questioning.

For wait, the same recall enables a fitting continuation, but leaves initiative timing and act legality with their current owners. Do not force every elapsed-time event to produce speech, or call a conversational suggestion a completed activity. Distinct contribution/voice remains an evaluated product behavior, not a blacklist that rejects valid safety/comfort language. No synchronous semantic rewrite loop is proposed.

## Voice/initiative and output risks beyond retrieval

Author text currently mixes stable disposition and a situational private wish in the minimal fixture. Do not continually restore the opening wish as permanent mood. Maintain stable persona as author truth, current conditions/outcomes as sourced facts and private sketches as dated subjective history. If users need authored scene intentions separate from stable persona, first define that authoring requirement; a recall change should not invent the split or reset their declarations.

Repeated “我听着/你想说就说” can be appropriate once and monotonous over repeated turns. Product evaluation must read consecutive dialogue, not just JSON legality or length. Require a visible response to the latest meaning, voice difference across two authored personas, appropriate questioning/refusal and absence of unsupported presuppositions; avoid exact phrase or forced novelty tests. Record which packet evidence a failing answer actually received before blaming memory.

The new fact-composition narration preserves exact source attribution but restricts literary expressiveness (`architecture-accuracy-2026-10-01.md`, narrator capability). More character recall cannot repair a failed full-prose provider or give that narrator vocabulary it does not support. Distinguish NPC spoken voice, chosen observable gestures and narration richness in review. Historical deterministic repeated nods demonstrate witnessed behavior, not rich literary success.

Missing author data must be surfaced to the creator through readiness and to product troubleshooting through bounded public request/configuration status, not explained as an NPC being intentionally silent. No private persona text should leak into those statuses. A test-ready fixture enables evaluation; real-world declarations remain their author's authority. Historical binaries and current source are separate evidence; use runtime revision plus actual provider receipts for any subsequent product claim.

## Finite proposed delivery and evidence

The implementation boundary is the existing private/history readers, context types/selection metadata, provider context serialization and their focused recovery/privacy tests. It changes read selection, not decision authority, world goals, narrative Event generation or controller ownership. Query-cost risk remains: authorized group counting scales with history. Measure the new peer query on the long fixture before claiming a latency improvement; bounded returned candidates alone are not constant database cost.

First prove the structural acceptance cases locally against exact ledger fixtures, actual provider projection and restart. Then use the same explicitly authored ready fixture/model/stimuli for a bounded before/after human-readable conversation with real provider authorization from the parent. Record technical results and separate human judgments of continuity, voice and initiative; legal output is not a human pass. Keep original full32 and frozen Golden exits open, and do not publish a new world to disguise missing canon. This assignment executed none of those future checks and claims no product completion.
