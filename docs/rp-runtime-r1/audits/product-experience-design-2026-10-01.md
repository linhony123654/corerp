# Product experience design review — 2026-10-01

Grounded speech/gesture beats, varied source-entailing language, scoped recall and an explicitly authored ready Rongqing fixture are a credible route to finished R1, but adding those features is not itself acceptance. The player must experience a recognizable character who understands the actual question, carries a conversation through changes and consequences, and stays within what that character knows. More templates or longer text alone cannot establish that outcome.

This is an independent DESIGN review at repository HEAD `f236c69`, based on local source, current architecture and checked-in real short outputs. No code, tests, runtime/provider calls, installs or plan edits were performed. Only this design report was created. Historical runs remain historical; no preview or current real-provider performance is verified here.

## Necessary observable product criteria

Use one authored character version, one documented model/configuration and the actual Play surface. These are proposed acceptance criteria, not claims of current passes.

1. **Character and relevance:** the character uses the declared relationship/address naturally, answers the question's qualifications, can disagree/refuse for a supplied reason, and does not revert to repeated greeting or generic reassurance. Human review distinguishes recognizable voice from merely mentioning the correct name. Unknown backstory stays unknown; plausible literary priors do not fill it.
2. **Conversation changes:** a request, hesitation, refusal, correction or subject switch materially changes the next response. At least one thread develops beyond repeating the player's sentiment. A spoken invitation never moves the player or supplies their consent.
3. **Continuity:** an authored commitment or witnessed action can be revisited after intervening turns and a paraphrased reference; subsequent cancellation, qualification or actual completion takes precedence. Another character who did not hear it does not know it. NPC memory and public narration do not share private intent.
4. **Embodied expression:** speech and a gesture feel like one beat when appropriate; gestures are optional and vary with context rather than adding a smile/nod every turn. Actor, target and event order remain unambiguous. Any consequential object, movement or activity goes through its existing typed owner; saying it is not doing it.
5. **Presentation and usability:** prose can connect actual beats with natural rhythm and meaningful style variation without padding, repeated location headers, hidden thoughts or new scene objects. Exact spoken text survives. The player can understand interruption versus chosen silence and continue/recover without duplicate effects. Test actual interaction latency/longest stalls rather than claiming improvement from configuration or two runs.

## Three key changes, ordered by information gained

### 1. Finish one explicitly authored playable scene and its evaluation contract

Use the existing authored creation/identity path to define a small Rongqing adaptation: a clear persona, directional relationship/address, public expression cues, a concrete present situation and a few lawful things the characters can actually do. Declare the authored version and any invented details openly in project-local content. Keep published cues separate from secrets. A full cast is unnecessary before a two-character scene works; add a third character only to test interaction/knowledge boundaries.

The scene should contain a conversational tension rather than an instruction to perpetually comfort the player. For example, one character wants to hear a concern while respecting a request not to press for its cause; later a different obligation or the player's changed request requires a choice. Any obligation must be authored/sourced, not assumed from the novel. This produces a testable change in behavior without a new goal engine.

Record expected known/unknown facts and what the player can observe before implementation. Inspect the actual provider view for a failing beat, not only its canonical builder. A READY receipt certifies required data presence, not good dialogue. Run a short real slice and review it before widening architecture. This content work is a product prerequisite, not a workaround that turns old missing-data Golden green.

### 2. Extend the existing observable/presentation contract to expressive beats

Keep the actor's model-written speech as a private proposal until validated/committed; keep gesture codes/targets typed and public only after witness projection. The existing speech-plus-expression atomic batch is already a useful minimal beat. Improve its contextual choice and support only demonstrably needed missing gestures or existing owner actions; do not start by adding unlimited gesture prose or multi-step planning.

For narration, preserve immutable speech/action atoms and actor/target labels, but support multiple source-entailing realizations and connective choices keyed to those atoms. Finite server-owned phrase variants can express cadence, simultaneous same-batch speech/gesture where justified, and publicly authored style without creating new actions. An optional connector must not assert elapsed time, causation, emotion or consent unsupported by its source. “He nodded” cannot become “he agreed”; an unordered fact collection cannot become “after” without chronology. Keep source attribution and coverage for each emitted group, including replay.

This is a source-safe expressive increment inside the current renderer, not a second narrator/world subsystem. It has a ceiling: if the variation remains mechanically templated, report that honestly and assess it with the player; do not market an enlarged template catalog as free literary prose. Any future free connective text needs an explicit weaker guarantee or a structurally constrained realization contract. A generic semantic entailment checker cannot prove arbitrary Chinese prose and should not be presented as doing so.

### 3. Make scoped recall succeed in the demonstrated scene before adding new memory state

Use the current heard/self dialogue groups and applied private sketches, prioritizing the exact authored conversational thread and its source evidence under the existing budget. Test paraphrased returns, qualification and topic switches rather than only “what did you just promise?”. Inspect actual omission/retrieval behavior when a recall fails. Fix candidate grouping/ranking or evidence availability where that is the cause; do not increase all limits indiscriminately.

Only if a real scene loses a continuing intention despite sufficient authorized evidence should a bounded applied-record continuation/revision mechanism be considered. Likewise, memory of a promise must not declare its fulfillment: use the actual task/activity/object owner outcome when relevant. Scoped recall is required for R1; a general goal lifecycle, embeddings, generated biographies and omniscient conversation summaries are not prerequisites established by these samples.

## Risky shortcuts to reject

- More template names without recognizable phrasing/rhythm or meaningful character response; more gesture codes without contextual selection; longer output as a proxy for RP.
- Hard rejecting ordinary dialogue using a broad model/regex judgment of “relevance”, “persona consistency” or “emotion correctness”. Retain deterministic hard checks for lawful effects, actor/target, permissions, freshness, provenance and visibility. Use semantic review/repair hints with bounded attempts and retained failures; test false positives. An unreliable automatic blocker can silence the RP it is intended to improve.
- Supplying assumed kinship, illness, history, relationship or scenery through literary prior, or allowing narrator prose to fulfill promises and enact player agency.
- Treating local branch publication as preview delivery, replacing frozen samples, changing model/input and attributing the result solely to runtime, or turning timeout fallback into a successful experience score.

## Real acceptance evidence and completion order

First collect a fixed 8-turn real authored slice covering relevance, refusal/qualification, one witnessed gesture and a return to an earlier thread. Read public output as a player, and inspect actual authorized packet/effect receipts privately for specific failures. Save both successes and failures; no curated transcript of only attractive replies. This is a proposed diagnostic size, not a replacement for the original acceptance suite.

Then run a real sustained 24–32-turn session with topic diversion, paraphrased recall, changed/cancelled commitment, one typed consequence, a third character with different knowledge and restart/recovery. This product transcript supplements the original unchanged technical 32 and its interaction/fault/postchecks; it must not substitute for them. Keep input/model/configuration fixed across comparisons, record latency/stalls/attempts and fallback occurrence, and verify no duplicated effects on recovery.

Use blinded paired human review of the same authored inputs for voice, relevance, continuity, expression, natural prose and agency/knowledge integrity. Record the user's rubric and decision explicitly; this review should not invent a numeric score threshold or declare a human pass. A technically lawful refusal or silence can be good RP; a transport-induced silence is a separate failure.

Finally prepare the actual preview from the verified executable, frontend, authored world and configuration, with release identity and a public smoke transcript. Publication/deployment authority is the parent's responsibility; this DESIGN review performs neither. Finished R1 requires implemented product behavior, passed world-integrity exit, approved sustained experience and accurately reported delivery status. Current v1 is a trustworthy starting point for that work, not its completion.

## Review of proposed natural default, without an extra narrator HTTP call

The proposed direction is feasible for finished conversational RP: a default server renderer can keep the player's attention on model-written, accepted character speech and committed scene beats, with optional model selection of public presentation. An additional narrator call is not inherently necessary. This can be materially better than v1's audit-like atoms without granting prose authority over facts. It does not promise unrestricted novel narration, and that limitation should remain explicit where requested.

The design is still too narrow if “natural” only removes time/place prefixes or cycles through synonyms while NPCs repeat reassurance and append nods. The short public samples' central weakness is thin conversational development; a prettier paragraph cannot fix it. The acceptance target must include a response that changes because of the player's qualification, a character-specific refusal/choice and a later consequence or revised stance—not a longer version of the same promise.

### Feasible architecture inside the existing boundary

1. **Decision owns substance.** Continue one authorized NPC packet and one existing validated decision/atomic batch. Accepted speech provides lexical character voice; optional typed gesture is chosen for a contextual reason, not required each turn. Expose only scene-required existing lawful owner choices. No free gesture sentence, actor reassignment, player movement/consent or inferred book history. Use actual packet/failure inspection to tune decision instructions on fixed authored content. The public renderer must not polish away a bad accepted reply; improve that reply upstream.
2. **Build a public beat sequence from committed evidence.** The renderer groups by actual actor, decision lineage, source turn and chronological sequence, not by heuristic textual proximity. Render speech and expression in their real public order. Same batch/time alone does not authorize concurrency; two neighboring events alone do not authorize causation. Parent's correction is right: “一边…一边…” and “听见这话，便…” need explicit supported temporal/causal semantics, not merely a shared batch. Without them, adjacency with a sentence break is natural and sufficient. A gesture preceding speech stays before it; quote-first is a preference for eligible beats, not permission to reverse public order.
3. **Realize the sequence naturally by default.** Keep exact source speech with an immutable actor binding. Use the canonical actor's label on the first utterance and whenever ambiguity arises; avoid rewriting that into an unbound pronoun. In a single-actor contiguous beat, vary finite source-entailing expression realization and punctuation, combine compatible adjacent atoms into short paragraphs, suppress redundant “回应/说” markers and repeated place/time headers. Preserve refusal meaning even when the spoken words are equivocal. Gesture alternatives must express the same directed/undirected action, not inferred agreement, confidence or intimacy. Present sourced place/time at scene changes or when useful rather than every line. Do not add atmosphere unless the authored/current public input actually supplies it.
4. **Use one shared realization path for default, optional composition and saved/replayed output.** An optional provider may select rhythm/template/focus among public choices; it should not be required to make the default pleasant. A source recipe identifies the finite choices and fact groups; final artifacts retain exact lines and provenance so restart never rerolls a variant. Stable selection may be keyed to the frozen turn/input/style. Do not draw wording from mutable random state or current identity/position during replay. These are extensions of the current renderer/receipt path, not a second runtime/world state.

### Concrete qualitative difference to demonstrate

For the same public facts—accepted speech from the NPC and a later directed beckon—v1 reads as separate status atoms such as “贾母 回应：「…」” then “贾母 向你招了招手。” A natural output can keep the exact utterance and binding, then render “贾母：「…」\n她向你招手。” only if the pronoun is unambiguous in that grouped actor scope. It must not become “她笑着拉你坐下”, “她边说边招手”, or “听你诉苦便招手安慰”: those introduce an extra act, concurrency or a causal/mental interpretation. If scope is ambiguous, repeat “贾母” rather than gamble on “她”. This improves rhythm and reduces bureaucratic prose while leaving substantive voice entirely in committed speech.

Parent should compare complete transcripts using the natural default against v1 on the same fixed authored inputs. Human evidence should identify concrete differences: less repetitive framing, readable multi-person speaker tracking, context-appropriate optional gestures, a distinct character voice in the actual dialogue, and a conversation that progresses rather than loops. Review a refusal, interruption, topic switch and ambiguous multi-actor beat—not just a flattering greeting. A natural default with fewer HTTP calls may improve responsiveness, but measure actual request timings and stalls rather than infer stable speed or fun from architecture. If these comparisons remain sterile, the first remedy is stronger sourced decision substance and authored scene tension; an unconstrained narrator is not the automatic next step.

This proposal can meet R1's conversational experience scope provided the sustained real transcript and user's qualitative acceptance succeed alongside unchanged integrity checks. It should not be described as general literary freedom. No automatic fun score or unproved semantic-entailment blocker is recommended.
