# RP-5 small / rare remembered visits

Status: source readers, default-off policy, private stable draws, shared pressure and optional actual HOT movement are implemented. Actual ordinary/rare hit/miss, temporary road obstruction, rollback, restart/rebuild, same-hour reuse and later contact pressure have focused tests. Large-scale and long-run frequency/path acceptance remain open; this is not complete RP-5 acceptance.

## Source choices

- Small candidate: revisit a public place the actor actually occupied, using immutable own agent movement plus active place-definition evidence. Exclude current place, homes, workplaces and entries outside the configured history. Keep at most16 distinct recent public destinations.
- Rare candidate: consider visiting a public place where the actor last actually encountered a known trusted person (own trust≥2, tension0, relationship source). Require at least seven full world days since that last encounter, an in-window own observation, and a destination different from the actor's current place. Keep at most16 candidates in stable oldest-encounter/friend-ID order. The threshold is eligibility, not a scheduled seven-day event or promise of reunion.

Select the latest actual encounter before checking whether its place is active/public. A recent private or closed-place encounter must not cause fallback to an older public encounter and fabricate a long absence. Actual social/hearing observations count as encounters, not only the mutable presence claim. Core validates actor, source IDs, date/gap and matching own relationship evidence; storage binds immutable observations/movements to the correct branch.

Never look up the friend's current position, future appointment or private residence to pick a destination. A remembered cafe may be empty or the friend may be elsewhere. Population materialization does not invent an arrival or new friendship. No health, cash, employment, access right or meeting guarantee is inferred.

## Verified dependency

Real conserved Nora background, player gifts and moves establish own trust and cafe memory. Nora's actual schedule takes her home; player remains elsewhere. Before seven days only own familiar cafe is eligible, at exactly seven days the remembered friend/cafe becomes eligible. Remote player movement back to the cafe does not update Nora's source. Reopen/rebuild retains sources; real private-place reencounter removes the old-friend eligibility and cannot resurrect an older cafe memory. History expiry excludes old sources. Terminal normal/race results and the corrected expiry-clock fixture are in progress.

## Runtime contract

Existing bounded HOT initiative/Wait authority owns execution; no global NPC model loop is added. Initial immutable policy accepts `visit_basis_points` (0–5000) and `rare_visit_basis_points` (0–100), both omitted/zero by default. Positive rare chance requires `history_hours`≥168 (existing maximum720). Rare receives no quiet/seeking bonus or event floor. Source selection prefers one eligible rare remembered friend, otherwise one ordinary remembered place if that family is enabled; an evaluated rare miss cannot fall back to ordinary. The actor's first visit receipt for the whole UTC hour is retained, including source, kind, original time and misses, across sessions/restarts or subsequent route/source changes.

Draw identity uses existing policy stream, actor, hash of scoped own source and hour. Contact→store→work→community→visit evaluation shares selected-offer history, including earlier offers in the same Wait. All selected visit offers count once even if the actor chooses silence. Cooldown, recent offer density, own major changes and current/imminent work suppress chance. Repeated selected kind/place/friend is suppressed throughout source history. Minor quiet bonus uses the existing mature-history rule; social-seeking intent does not boost visits. Public Wait broadcasts and provider context exclude raw draw/seed; the provider receives only own remembered source and selection.

Proposal remains optional: own personality/urgent needs, current/imminent work, law and actual traversability take precedence. Require a directly linked remembered destination and immediate physical traversal at Wait's target world time. This is checked independently of the persisted pre-Wait clock, including road works beginning/ending during the interval. Existing initiative context/commit rechecks current routes. A multi-step excursion still needs a sourced continuation contract, not teleportation or untracked intent. Existing movement/route/hearing/replay owns effects. No fake dialogue, wage or new relationship merely from selecting an opportunity. If earlier same-Wait effects supersede the actor's last encounter, context marks the original pinned source unselected instead of rerolling or failing the whole Wait.
