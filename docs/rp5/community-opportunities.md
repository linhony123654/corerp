# RP-5 community change source boundary

Status: known-source reader plus default-off draw/pressure/initiative slice implemented and actual repeal→speech-or-quiet→hearing/recovery tested. This covers local rule changes, not every neighborhood event or full RP-5 acceptance.

Do not treat `RPBackgroundMaterialized` as a new resident moving in. It initializes an existing conserved person's background; calling that a migration would invent a world event. No unsourced new resident, fabricated illness or decorative festival is added here.

First community source reuses actual regional rule amendments/repeals, including existing enactment, effective time, public announcement/hearing and limited NPC knowledge. It is a change in local community rules, not all possible neighborhood change kinds.

`readRPCommunityChangeSources` selects only the actor's actual current place and legally known regional rules. First choose the latest effective known version per institution/rule, then require a revision/predecessor and local scope. A repeal remains a real change even though it no longer prohibits speech. Initial rules, future versions, distant/world-wide rules and unheard authoritative changes do not qualify. Unheard later authoritative revisions must not rewrite the actor's incomplete beliefs.

Age is anchored to `max(first_actual_knowledge_time, effective_time)`. Knowledge before activation becomes relevant only at activation; hearing an already-effective rule becomes relevant when actually heard. Repeated announcements cannot reset first knowledge identity or refresh an expired source. A later-heard version cannot suppress an older version in an earlier knowledge-time window. Source output retains first Knowledge Event, enactment/predecessor, effective time, region places and known text; no random stream or managerial private fact is read. Reads produce no Event, Knowledge or effect.

Actual evolution fixture now checks unknown sources, no initial/future revision at13:00, amendment at14:00, repeat announcement at14:30 failing to renew a14:15 history start, repeal at15:00, and stable recovery after reopen/rebuild. Core checks cover exact activation, scope, incomplete knowledge, missing knowledge provenance, repeal and caller slice ownership. Terminal results are in project progress.

## Runtime slice

`policy.community_basis_points` (0–5000, default0) opts in on immutable policy installation. Empty/default values preserve legacy hashes. Completed Wait evaluates at most one latest eligible local change per HOT actor, after contact/store/work, and pins private actor/enactment/hour draw receipts. Same-hour results, including misses, are reused; a selected source is suppressed throughout the history window. Original receipt time supplies shared cooldown/pressure with contact/store/work. The known change itself excludes a quiet-history bonus, including for other personal opportunity families; no generic social-seeking boost is applied.

Initiative construction rechecks the receipt against actual own first-hearing/version/locality evidence. Providers receive only the already-known law and selection flag, never stream, roll, chance or raw pressure. Deterministic sociable/free characters may discuss a selected local change; urgent goals, lawful-action constraints and weather retreat remain prior choices. Selection does not force speech. A contact miss cannot suppress this independent selected source. Wait broadcasts omit community receipts.

Real integration deliberately selects hit/miss seeds for the same authored local repeal, reopens/rebuilds before initiative, executes deterministic speech or silence, verifies actual player hearing, prevents repeated provider work, checks same-hour receipt identity and subsequent same-source cooldown. Later real gifts create friendship eligibility and check that the community selection affects contact pressure. This is actual behavior/recovery evidence, not statistical long-run divergence or live-model verification.

This integration exposed a prior deterministic initiative bug: any next work appointment, even tomorrow, suppressed free-time social choices. The rule now suppresses discretionary choices only while working or when the next work appointment is within one hour (invalid times fail closed), matching existing opportunity eligibility. Core regression covers exact one-hour/+one-second, overdue and next-day work. Initial community hit failure and corrected verification are recorded in progress.

Small and rare opportunities, wider neighborhood changes, scale and long-run divergence remain explicit RP-5 requirements.
